## 💡 Quick Study Tips for BPSC TRE
- **Notice Option 'D' & 'E':** In BPSC TRE exams, Option D ("उपर्युक्त में से एक से अधिक / More than one of the above") and Option E ("उपर्युक्त में से कोई नहीं / None of the above") account for nearly 15-20% of answers. Always evaluate every option carefully before marking.
- **Negative Marking Alert:** There is a 1/3 negative marking penalty. Do not guess blindly unless you can eliminate at least two options.
- **Language Qualifying:** 30 marks total (minimum 9 marks required to pass). While non-merit, failing this disqualifies your entire paper!
